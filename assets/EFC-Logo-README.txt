CHOOSE THE RIGHT FORMAT
PNG: everyday use in Google Slides, Canva, documents, chat and email.
The background is transparent. Avoid enlarging beyond its native resolution.

SVG: a real vector file that stays sharp at any size. Use it for websites,
design work, and handoff to a designer, print supplier or production partner.
If your application does not support SVG, use the PNG instead.

CHOOSE THE RIGHT COLOUR
- Dark blue: white or light backgrounds.
- White: dark or navy backgrounds.
- Light blue: dark blue backgrounds with good contrast.
